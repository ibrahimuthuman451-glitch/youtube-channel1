## This one is written around Video 1's failure

Video 1 measured **1:26 average view duration on 9:19 — 15.4%**. Its click-through was a
healthy 4.9%, so people were interested; they left almost immediately. Four structural
changes fix that here, and they are deliberate, not stylistic:

| Video 1 did this | Video 2 does this instead |
|---|---|
| 30s of thesis before a single fact | **A number in the first five seconds** — "$50 million", before any framing |
| Backstory (§ paper mill, 1865) at 1:19, right when viewers decide to stay | **No origin story at all.** Blockbuster's founding never appears |
| One hook, at the start | **Second hook at the midpoint** (§7, "Then the board acted" — 63 words, pure turn) |
| Ended on a reflection | Ends on a question aimed at the viewer: *your* late fee |

Word count is down from 1,361 to 1,138 on purpose. A 7:35 video that holds beats a 9:19
one that leaks — and once retention is healthy, length can grow again.

## Sourcing and honesty notes

Everything here is widely documented, but four figures are commonly repeated with slight
variation, and the script is already hedged where it matters:

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| Netflix offered itself for **$50M in 2000** | Widely reported (Hastings/Randolph accounts), not from SEC filings — treat as reported |
| **~9,000 stores, ~60,000 employees** | Peak figures, 2004-era; different sources cite 8,000–9,100 |
| **~$5.9B revenue** | 2004 fiscal year, widely cited |
| **Late fees "hundreds of millions"** | Reported at roughly $800M in *revenue*; the *profit* figure is smaller and varies. Script says "hundreds of millions… close to pure profit" — the hedge is deliberate |
| **Blockbuster Online "millions of subscribers"** | No exact number given on purpose; it was reportedly approaching 2M |
| **Dish paid ~$320M in 2011** | Accurate |
| **Last stores closed 2018 (Alaska)** | Accurate |
| **Qwikster: "hundreds of thousands" lost** | Reported ~800k; deliberately rounded down |
| **"Netflix moved to streaming in 2007"** | Watch Now launched that year; correct |

Deliberately omitted: Antioco's exact exit terms, Carl Icahn's stake, and the 2011 price
increase percentage — each adds a minute of exposition for a detail the argument doesn't
need.
