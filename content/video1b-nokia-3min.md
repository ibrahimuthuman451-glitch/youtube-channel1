## Why this exists

Video 1 measured **17 views, 4.9% CTR, and 1:26 average view duration on 9:19 — 15.4%**.
The click was fine; the viewing wasn't. Two causes are plausible and this cut addresses
both:

1. **It was long.** A 9-minute video from a channel with no track record asks for a lot of
   trust up front. 3 minutes asks for almost none.
2. **It front-loaded the thesis instead of the evidence.** Video 1 spent 30 seconds
   explaining its argument, then went to an 1865 paper mill at 1:19.

## The curiosity chain

Every section ends by opening the next question, and the hook's promise is not paid off
until the memo. That's the whole design:

| Section | Ends by making you ask… |
|---|---|
| Not the iPhone | "…then what was it?" — answers: *a memo* |
| The peak | "how does the biggest company in the world lose this?" |
| The trap | "…so they hired an outsider. Who?" |
| The memo | "…it leaked. What happened next?" |
| The fall | "…and yet?" (sets up the twist) |
| What survived | "so Nokia isn't dead?" |
| The question | turns it on the viewer |

Two open loops are deliberate: the hook names *a memo* without saying what it said, and
section 3 closes with "an outsider" without naming Elop for a full cycle.

## What was cut from the 9:19 version

- The 1865 paper-mill origin — entirely gone. It was the biggest single retention risk.
- The "three year project, not six months" explanation of Symbian debt.
- The developer-arithmetic passage, compressed into one sentence.
- The explicit "app gap was a trust failure" line is kept; the surrounding argument isn't.
- Antti Herlin / board-level detail, and the closing "fourteen years of runway" line.

Everything factual is carried over unchanged from Video 1 — including the hedges
("reportedly", "widely reported").

## Where it sits in the schedule

This is a **mid-form** video, not a Short: 3–4 minutes is a legitimate format of its own and
can carry the same thumbnail and title treatment. It can run as a re-cut of the Nokia story,
or as the pinned "start here" entry point that funnels into the 9-minute version later.
